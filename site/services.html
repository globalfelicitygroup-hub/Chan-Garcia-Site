Compliance Normativo

We evaluate the effectiveness of compliance programs against applicable laws, regulations, and internal policies — including fiscal and accounting compliance, anti-corruption, anti-money laundering, data protection, labor policies, and fraud prevention.

Area B

Corporate Governance

We analyze the governance structure: distribution of rights and responsibilities between the board, management, and shareholders. We evaluate decision-making protocols, committee effectiveness, oversight mechanisms, and succession planning against OECD and CCE standards.

Area C

Contracts & Legal Exposure

We review the contractual framework to detect exposure levels, risk concentrations, and structural vulnerabilities — including supplier agreements, intercompany arrangements, and third-party obligations that create quantifiable financial exposure.

All legal, governance, and compliance findings are translated into financial terms using professional actuarial methodology. Scenario modeling produces probability-weighted exposure ranges — converting abstract legal concepts into actionable financial metrics.

Methodology

From risk identification to financial quantification

Every assessment follows the same five-phase methodology — producing comparable, auditable outputs that institutional committees can reference and defend.

01

Scope & Framework Definition

Define the assessment scope, identify applicable regulatory frameworks, and select reference standards appropriate to the entity's sector, jurisdiction, and corporate structure in Mexico.

02

Document Review & Interviews

Gather evidence through analysis of governance documents, compliance policies, organizational structures, codes of ethics, and contractual frameworks. Conduct structured interviews with directors, board members, and key function owners.

03

Control Testing

Verify the operational effectiveness of internal controls through design and operational testing — including segregation of duties, data protection measures, and compliance enforcement mechanisms.

04

Actuarial Financial Quantification

Translate all legal, governance, and compliance findings into financial terms using professional actuarial methodology. Run scenario models with multiple methodologies to produce probability-weighted exposure ranges.

05

Defensible Report & Briefing

Deliver an executive-grade report with findings, governance scores, exposure heatmaps, quantified financial ranges, and a prioritized action plan — supported by recognized legal and governance reference frameworks.

Standards and frameworks: Our methodology is grounded in international compliance standards, OECD governance principles, Mexico's CCE Code of Best Practices for Corporate Governance, and sector-specific regulatory requirements. Assessments are calibrated to the applicable legal framework for each jurisdiction, sector, and corporate structure — ensuring outputs are defensible, comparable, and recognized by institutional stakeholders.
